🏆 Achievement

Bringing Library Hub Resources Closer to Teachers and Learners

The CID–LRMS team visited Agnay ES and Agnipa ES as part of the Division Reading Program, Project GRACE (Grow in Reading Attitude, Comprehension, and Enthusiasm), launching the SMART-LR: Library HUB-it Initiative to promote reading, storytelling, and access to quality learning resources.

The initiative encourages book borrowing, storytelling sessions, and the growth of reading spaces and school libraries division-wide — with the Romblon East Central School Library Hub continuing to manage supplementary learning resources for public schools across Romblon, Corcuera, Banton, and Concepcion.

    💛 About

    Built by the Division, for every school

    The LRMS Portal is maintained by the CID – Learning Resource Management Section (LRMS) of SDO Romblon, gathering self-learning modules, supplementary materials, and links to regional and national learning-resource systems in one place for teachers and learners across the division.

    The Romblon East Central School Library Hub helps manage supplementary learning resources for public schools across the districts of Romblon, Corcuera, Banton, and Concepcion.

    Administered by
    CID – Learning Resource Management Section, SDO Romblon
    Coverage
    Kindergarten through Grade 12, plus ALS and SHS programs
    Format
    Self-learning modules, activity sheets, slide decks, video lessons, e-learning links
    Cost
    Free for all Romblon public schools
